Many hosters download without JDownloader, with their countdown and captcha shown in this app, where captchas always pop up. You can put links in a category by hand, and the browser extension puts a download button on videos. KnightLoader has a redrawn logo and follows GlimStone 4.3.1: the sign-in is one card, and every list trembles while you move one of its items.
